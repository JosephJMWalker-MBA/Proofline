## Citation boundary

These sources establish adjacent prior art in provenance, digital-evidence reproducibility, government-information retrieval, municipal-document extraction, evidence attribution, epistemic action selection, and version-aware document understanding. They do **not** establish that Proofline is novel as a whole.

The first Proofline report therefore makes only the narrower contribution claim documented in `RELATED_WORK_BOUNDARY.md`: executable source-authority constraints, exact benchmark identity frozen before scoring, transfer across two materially different municipal publisher stacks, and non-transfer of human judgment when deterministic evidence-derived identity changes.
